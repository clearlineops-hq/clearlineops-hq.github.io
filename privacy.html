Privacy Policy

Clearline Operations Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy explains, in plain English, what information Clearline Operations ("Clearline," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website, our lead and request forms, email with us, and any conversation samples you share with us for a review, such as the free 25-conversation health check.

Clearline Operations is based in Kansas, USA.

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to respond to you and deliver what you asked for.
  • We use conversation samples only to deliver your review.
  • We never sell your information.
  • We delete review data within 30 days after your review is delivered, unless you become a retainer client.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@clearlineopshq.com.

What we collect

1. Website. When you visit our website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time of your visit. We use it to keep the site running and secure and to understand overall traffic. We don't use it to build advertising profiles.

2. Lead and request forms. When you download a resource (such as the AI Support Health-Check) or request a review, we collect what you enter. That's usually your work email and, optionally, your company name and details about your request.

3. Email. When you email us, we receive your name, email address, and anything you include in your message or attachments.

4. Conversation samples for reviews. When you request a health check or other review, you may share transcripts of conversations between your AI support agent and your customers. We ask you to remove or mask customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, and other personal information before sending. If we find personal information you missed, we'll tell you and delete that file.

We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our services are intended for businesses.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • send you the resource you requested;
  • respond to your questions and requests;
  • review your conversation samples and prepare your report;
  • follow up about your request and, if you're interested, our services (you can tell us to stop at any time); and
  • keep our website and email secure and working properly.

Conversation samples are used only to deliver your review. We don't use them for marketing, we don't publish them, and we don't use them to train AI models. We won't quote your conversations or name your company publicly without your written permission.

How we share your information

We never sell or rent your information.

We share information only:

  • with service providers that help us run our business, such as our email, file-storage, website-hosting, and form providers, and only so far as they need it to provide those services to us;
  • when required by law, for example to respond to a valid legal request; or
  • with your permission.

How long we keep it

  • Conversation samples and review working files: deleted within 30 days after your review is delivered, unless you become a retainer client. If you become a retainer client, we keep them for as long as we work together and delete them within 30 days after the engagement ends, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • Your contact details and email correspondence: kept for as long as needed to respond to you and for ordinary business records, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Website logs: kept for a limited period by our hosting provider for security and operations.

If you shared conversations through a folder link you control, you can also revoke our access yourself at any time.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Those steps include limiting who can access review files, storing them with reputable service providers, and asking you to redact customer data before you share it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it, including any conversation samples you've shared; and
  • opt out of follow-up emails by replying "unsubscribe" or emailing us.

To make any of these requests, email hello@clearlineopshq.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time, and normally within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them as required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the effective date at the top. If we make a significant change to how we use review data, we'll tell clients with active reviews directly.
