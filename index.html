AI Agent Tune-Up

AI support agents rarely fail on day one. They drift: pricing changes and the help center doesn't, handoffs loop, and the bot guesses instead of saying "I don't know." Find out what yours is actually telling customers.

Start here: the free 25-conversation health check

Share 25 real, redacted conversations from your AI support agent or chatbot. We review them and send a short, scored report with specific examples and a prioritized fix list, within 5 business days of receiving your conversations. Free, with no obligation to buy anything.

The 5 areas we score

1. Resolution rate

How many conversations were fully resolved by the agent, how many needed a human, and how many ended unresolved.

2. Wrong or made-up answers

Outdated or invented answers, contradictions of your published policies, and promises the agent had no authority to make.

3. Missed handoffs to humans

Loops, ignored requests for a person, and handoffs that lost context along the way.

4. Tone

Replies that are off-brand, too long, too stiff, or poorly matched to the customer's mood.

5. Knowledge-base gaps

Questions the agent couldn't answer because the information was missing, out of date, or out of reach.

Plus

Redacted examples from your own conversations, your top 3 to 5 fixes ranked by impact and effort, and a one-paragraph summary for leadership.

Want it fixed for you?

The AI Agent Tune-Up is an optional, done-for-you monthly retainer for ongoing monitoring and tuning of your AI support agent.

  • Ongoing monitoring of real conversations: resolution rate, wrong answers, and escalations over time.
  • Knowledge upkeep as prices, policies, and products change.
  • Handoff and guardrail tuning so customers reach a person when they should, with firm limits on refunds, promises, and sensitive data.
  • Tone tuning for reply length, voice, and upset customers.
  • Fixes applied every cycle, with a short report on what changed and why.
